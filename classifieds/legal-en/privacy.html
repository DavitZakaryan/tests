Privacy Policy

Operated by ARMI7 INC dba HayList · Glendale, California · Effective November 17, 2026 · Version 1

HayList is an online classifieds service operated by ARMI7 INC dba HayList ("HayList", "we"). This Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using HayList you agree to this Policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and password (stored hashed) when you register.
  • Listing content — the ads you post: text, photos, price, city, address you choose to show, and your contact details shown in the ad.
  • Communications — messages you send through the service and reports you file.
  • Usage and device data — pages viewed, searches, IP address, browser type, and similar technical data, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use information

  • To operate the service: publish your listings, show your contact details to other users, and connect buyers and sellers.
  • To moderate content, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms.
  • To communicate with you about your account and listings.
  • To improve the service and understand how it is used.

Sharing

Information you put in a listing (including your phone number and address, if you add them) is public. We share data with service providers that help us run the site (hosting, analytics, payments) only as needed, and when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Your California privacy rights

California residents may request to know what personal information we hold about them, request deletion, and correct inaccurate data. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, email davit@sitvio.com. We will respond as required by the CCPA/CPRA.

Retention and security

We keep account and listing data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for legal and anti-fraud purposes. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards; no system is perfectly secure.

Children

HayList is for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes and contact
