Prohibited Items & Conduct

Operated by ARMI7 INC dba HayList · Glendale, California · Effective November 17, 2026 · Version 1

To keep HayList safe and lawful, the following may not be listed, sold, or promoted on the service. We remove violating listings and may ban accounts without notice.

Prohibited items

  • Anything illegal under federal, California, or local law
  • Firearms, ammunition, explosives, and weapon parts regulated by law
  • Illegal drugs, controlled substances, and drug paraphernalia
  • Stolen property, burglary tools, and items with removed serial numbers
  • Counterfeit goods and unauthorized copies (including fake documents, IDs, diplomas, and license plates)
  • Government documents, personal data, mailing lists, and financial credentials
  • Hazardous, recalled, or unsafe products (including recalled child products)
  • Tobacco, vaping, and alcohol listings that do not comply with age and licensing law
  • Live animals offered in violation of law; commercial puppy/kitten sales; wild or protected species
  • Human remains, body parts, and surrogacy or adoption offers
  • Lottery tickets, gambling services, and unregistered securities or crypto investment schemes
  • Adult services, escort services, and any content involving minors in any sexual context (zero tolerance; reported to authorities)

Prohibited conduct

  • Fraud, scams, bait-and-switch, and advance-fee schemes
  • Posting the same item in multiple categories or cities to spam the feed
  • Harassment, hate content, or discrimination in listings
  • Collecting other users' data or contacting them for unrelated marketing
  • Impersonating another person or business

Reporting

To report a violation, email davit@sitvio.com with a link to the listing. In an emergency or if a crime is in progress, call 911.
