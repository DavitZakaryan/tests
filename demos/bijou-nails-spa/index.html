About Us

A Family Touch

Bijou Nails & Spa is a family-run salon that has been caring for Glendale's hands and feet since 2015. Tucked along North Brand Boulevard, we keep things simple: a calm, elegant space, careful work, and a warm welcome every time you walk in.
