Our Story

Baked fresh, the Armenian way

Zhengyalov Hatz brings one of Armenia's most beloved breads to Broadway in Glendale. Our signature zhengyalov hatz (Ժենգյալով հաց) is a thin, griddled flatbread stuffed with a generous mix of fresh chopped herbs — rolled, baked to order, and served warm, just like back home.

Stop by for lunch, pick up fresh baked goods for the family, or grab an Armenian coffee to go — everything is made in-house, every day.
