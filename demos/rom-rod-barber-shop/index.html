About Us

A Real Barbershop on Glenoaks

Rom-Rod Barber Shop is a classic neighborhood barbershop on West Glenoaks Blvd in Glendale. We focus on the fundamentals — precise cuts, clean fades and sharp beard work — in a laid-back shop where you are never rushed out of the chair. Walk-ins welcome, appointments by phone.
