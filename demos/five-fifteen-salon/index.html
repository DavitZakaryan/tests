About Us

A calm, light-filled studio in Glendale

Five Fifteen Salon is a hair & makeup studio on East Glenoaks Boulevard — a relaxed space where great hair never feels rushed. Every appointment starts with a conversation about your hair, your routine and the look you want to leave with.

You'll be in good hands with stylists Dina & Robert — known for careful cuts, dimensional color and a friendly, personal approach. It's the kind of care that keeps guests coming back — and rating us 4.9 stars.
